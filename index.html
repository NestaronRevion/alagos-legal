Events and sign-ups

Post an event in a minute. Members sign up with one press.

Pick a template or start fresh. Alagos posts the event with its banner, sign-up buttons and a list that updates itself, sends the reminders, and opens attendance when it starts.

  • Your own answers, like Starter, Sub and Won't Attend, each with its own limit
  • Role slots such as Tank, Healer and DPS, with a bench that moves people up by itself
  • Weekly, every other day or on the days you choose, and skip a week when you need to
  • Reminders, a sign-up deadline and a discussion thread for each event
  • Officers edit the event or take attendance straight from the post

The event editor

Every setting on a tab, every change on a button or a form. Nothing changes until you press Save, and the editor lists what you're about to change.

Why Alagos

Built by an officer, for officers.

Alagos grew out of running a real league every week. It does the jobs officers end up doing by hand in spreadsheets and pinned messages.

Signing up isn't attending

Officers confirm who really came, from a quick checklist. Reports, loot points and rotations all count real attendance.

Buttons, not commands

A command opens something; after that, everything happens on buttons and forms. Nobody has to memorise syntax or template IDs.

Fair, and visibly so

Rotations, benches and loot follow rules everyone can see, so the same people don't always get picked or always sit out.

One bot instead of several

Events, attendance, rosters, loot, roles, welcomes and reports work together, so they share the same roster and the same history.

See it in Discord

Panels your members and officers already know how to use.

Alagos posts a panel of buttons in your server. Everything a button opens is private to the person who pressed it.

What it does

Everything a guild runs on, in one place.

Events and sign-ups

  • Event posts with sign-up buttons, reminders and a discussion thread
  • Repeating events, your own event types, and ready-made templates picked by name
  • Role slots, like Tank, Healer and DPS, with a bench that moves people up by itself
  • A temporary role for everyone signed up, removed after the event
  • Scheduled announcements, once or on a repeat
  • Old event messages cleared away once an event is over, if you want

Attendance and reports

  • Confirmed attendance from name ranges or a search, in seconds
  • Reports by member, by event type and week by week
  • Past events: who signed up and who came, for any event that has ended

Roster and characters

  • A roster with leave, notes and referrals
  • Members claim their character, or add their own where the game allows it
  • Mains and alts, with each game's own details
  • Personal reminders by DM that members control, with quiet hours

Loot

  • Soft reserve, loot council and wishlists
  • Points systems: DKP, Zero-Sum DKP and EPGP, with bidding, decay and a points board
  • Points that follow attendance, so a correction never leaves points behind

Your server

  • Member and officer panels: buttons for the things people do every week
  • Role panels, and roles given automatically when someone joins the roster
  • A welcome flow that walks newcomers through your rules, roles and characters
  • Permissions by Discord role, with ready-made bundles for admins and officers

Trust

  • Anonymous feedback that stores no trace of who sent it
  • An audit trail of who changed what, and when
  • Alagos only uses Discord's basic server access and cannot read your messages
  • A public Privacy Policy and Terms, and deletion on request
