Alagos for Sim Racing

Championships for any sim: Assetto Corsa Competizione, Le Mans Ultimate, iRacing, F1, Gran Turismo and the rest. A grid with full-timers and reserves, results typed in seconds, standings that keep themselves up to date, and stewards for incidents, all in your league's Discord. Switch it on in Guild Settings; it's free.

Championships

🏆 A season, set up in a few minutes

  • The sim and the format: race only, qualifying and a race, a sprint and a feature, or two races.
  • Classes on one grid (GT3 and GT4, Hypercar and LMP2), each with its own number of cars, and teams.
  • Entries: each driver with a car number, a team and a class, full-time, reserve or guest. Two to four drivers can share a car for endurance races.
  • Drivers add themselves with their Steam ID, iRacing ID or console name, and can ask to enter with one button.

Free: one running championshipPremium: several at once

The grid

🏎️ Full-timers first, reserves ready

  • Each round is an event with a sign-up button per class, reminders and confirmed attendance.
  • Full-time drivers keep their seat whenever they sign up; reserves fill the seats they give up, in order, and get a direct message when they move up.
  • A race ban keeps a driver off the next round's grid by itself.

Free

Results

🏁 Type the finish, see the points, save

  • One car a line: a number or a name, near spellings understood, with times if you have them. Then retirements, disqualifications, pole and fastest lap.
  • A preview first, with every car's points, before anything is saved. Saving marks everyone who raced as attending.
  • Points the usual ways: F1, IndyCar-style, MotoGP-style, or one point per car beaten; a sprint table; double points for a finale.

Free: the presetsPremium: your own table, bonus points, dropped rounds

Standings

📊 Standings that keep themselves

  • A standings post in your channel, redrawn a few seconds after every result or penalty.
  • Drivers per class, and teams, with dropped rounds and ties broken by countback.
  • Each driver's own results, round by round, and any round's full classification, a button away.

Free

Stewards

⚖️ Incidents, decided fairly

  • Report an incident from the round's post: the cars involved, when, what happened, a video link.
  • Each report in your stewards' channel with its own thread to discuss it.
  • Penalties: time, positions, points, disqualification, a grid penalty or a race ban, always with a reason the drivers read. They are told by direct message, and the decision can be posted for everyone.
  • A penalty never overwrites a result: remove it and the result is back as it was.
