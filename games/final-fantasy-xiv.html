Sign-ups

⚔️ Press a role; Alagos picks the job

  • Tank, Pure healer, Barrier healer, Melee, Ranged and Caster: the party a static builds. A member presses a role and is signed up as their main job if it plays it, else their second job, else another job they play.
  • The post shows the party: Wol · Scholar.
  • Simpler nights work too: on an event with one Healer or one DPS role, both kinds of healer fill Healer and every damage job fills DPS.
  • A fair bench in sign-up order, moving up by itself, with a DM when someone gets in.

Characters

🎭 One character, every job

  • The way the game works: a character has its World, a main job, a second job and every job it plays, ticked from all 21 combat jobs.
  • Item level, an FF Logs link and notes (clears, availability) for the officers.
  • Members add their own, main and alts, and officers can fix anyone's.
  • Evercold's jobs, Bastion and Zephyr, are added when it launches.

Duties

🔥 Savage, Ultimate and more, picked by name

  • Savage night, Ultimate prog and Extreme trial at 8: 2 tanks, a pure and a barrier healer, 2 melee, 1 ranged, 1 caster. Or a party with any 4 damage.
  • Alliance raids at 24, Deep Dungeon runs at 4 and treasure map parties.
  • The current Savage tier and every Ultimate in the game's list.
  • Your group is a Free Company on every screen; rename it to Static, or whatever you call yourselves.

Everything else

🎁 Loot, applications and your Raid-Helper history

  • Loot your way: soft reserve, loot council and wishlists free (a wishlist suits a static's Best-in-Slot list); DKP, Zero-Sum DKP and EPGP with Premium. Never gil: no auctions for in-game currency.
  • Applications to join: applicants give their character, World, jobs, clears and when they can raid. Accept, Accept with conditions, Bench or Deny, each with a thread for the officers.
  • Import from Raid-Helper, free: past events and their sign-ups come with you, each with its job and role (a White Mage as a pure healer).
