Sign-ups

⚔️ Press a role; Alagos picks the Discipline

  • Tank, Healer and DPS buttons (or Melee and Ranged too). A member presses one and is signed up with the Discipline of theirs that plays it: their main, then their off-Discipline, then their style's other one, on their main character first and then their alts.
  • The post shows the comp: Kallig · Corruption Sorcerer.
  • A fair bench in sign-up order, moving up by itself, with a DM when someone gets in.
  • Officers move people between roles and the bench, and confirm who really came.

Characters

🧙 Every Combat Style, Republic and Empire

  • All 16 Combat Styles, each faction's name its own (Sentinel and Marauder, Sage and Sorcerer), with their three Disciplines. The Discipline menu offers only that style's.
  • Members add their own characters, main and alts, with their server, item rating, a Parsely or Starparse logs link and a build or note.
  • Ready for 8.0: the Disciplines and Operations are data, so Masters of the Force's changes are an update to the list, not to your setup.

Operations

🛸 Operations, picked by name

  • Templates for Children of Nul, R-4 Anomaly, Dxun and Gods from the Machine at 8 (2 tanks, 2 healers, 4 damage) and 16 (2, 4, 10). Change the slots for any night.
  • Every Operation in the game's list for your own templates, from Eternity Vault to Children of Nul.
  • Flashpoint nights, Conquest pushes, PvP and world bosses as events of their own.

Everything else

🎁 Loot, applications and your Raid-Helper history

  • Loot your way: soft reserve, loot council and wishlists free; DKP, Zero-Sum DKP and EPGP with Premium, earned from confirmed attendance. Never credits: no auctions for in-game currency.
  • Applications to join: applicants give their character, server, Combat Style and Discipline, Operations experience and availability. Accept, Accept with conditions, Bench or Deny, each with a thread for the officers.
  • Import from Raid-Helper, free: past Operations and their sign-ups come with you. Raid-Helper records a Combat Style and a role; Alagos fills in the Discipline where only one of the style's plays that role.
