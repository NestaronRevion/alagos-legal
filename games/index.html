Games

Made for the games you play.

Events, sign-ups, attendance, rosters, loot and reports work for any game, with no module at all. A game module adds that game's own words, character details, event types and features. Switch modules on for your server in Guild Settings.

Available now

DC Dark Legion

  • Train and Guardian rotations that share the seats fairly, with rules you choose
  • City Hall and cannon teams, posted before each battle
  • Gotham and Krypton Rush seasons, with Red Box tracking
  • A live battle tracker for City Hall, including the Nexus
Everything it does

More games are on the way. Ask in the support server which ones, or request yours in the feature request forum.

Playing something else?

Alagos works without a game module: events, sign-ups with role slots, confirmed attendance, rosters, loot and reports are all there from the start.
