Games

Made for the games you play.

Events, sign-ups, attendance, rosters, loot and reports work for any game, with no module at all. A game module adds that game's own words, character details, event types and features. Switch modules on for your server in Guild Settings.

Available now

DC Dark Legion

  • Train and Guardian rotations that share the seats fairly, with rules you choose
  • City Hall and cannon teams, posted before each battle
  • Gotham and Krypton Rush seasons, with Red Box tracking
  • A live battle tracker for City Hall, including the Nexus
Everything it does Available now

World of Warcraft

  • Mists of Pandaria Classic, Midnight and WoW: Forever, each its own game
  • Raid sign-ups by role, with each member's spec picked for them
  • Every current raid as a template, picked by name
  • Characters with their realm, loot with tier tokens, applications to join
Everything it does Available now

Star Wars: The Old Republic

  • Operation sign-ups by Combat Style and Discipline
  • Every Operation, from Eternity Vault to Children of Nul, at 8 and 16
  • Characters with their server, Republic and Empire names both
  • Ready for Game Update 8.0, Masters of the Force
Everything it does Available now

Guild Wars 2

  • Squad sign-ups by Tank, Healer, Support or DPS, with the build you're playing
  • All nine professions and their elite specializations
  • Raids, Fractals, World vs World and meta nights
  • Account names, logs and build links on each character
Everything it does Available now

Final Fantasy XIV

  • Party sign-ups by job, pure and barrier healers as their own roles
  • One character with every job they play, and their World
  • Savage, Ultimate, Extreme and Alliance raids as templates
  • Wishlists for a static's Best-in-Slot, and applications to join
Everything it does Available now

Sim Racing

  • Championships for any sim: ACC, Le Mans Ultimate, iRacing, F1, Gran Turismo
  • A grid per class, full-timers first and reserves ready
  • Results typed in seconds, standings that keep themselves up to date
  • Incident reports and stewards, with penalties drivers can read
Everything it does Available now

Competitive/PvP (FPS & Esports)

  • CS2, Valorant, Overwatch 2, Marvel Rivals, Rainbow Six, CoD, Battlefield, battle royale
  • Scrims and matches by role, with substitutes
  • Your clan's record by map and opponent
  • Leagues with fixtures, results both teams confirm, and a table
Everything it does

More games are on the way. Ask in the support server which ones, or request yours in the feature request forum.

Every module also brings what the core does for any game: events and reminders, confirmed attendance, rosters, loot, reports, and a free import of your Raid-Helper history.

Playing something else?

Alagos works without a game module: events, sign-ups with role slots, confirmed attendance, rosters, loot and reports are all there from the start.
