Scrims and matches

🎯 Your five, your subs, sorted

  • Templates for every game: a CS2 five (Entry, AWPer, Lurker, Support, IGL), Valorant by role, Overwatch 1-2-2, Marvel Rivals 2-2-2, Rainbow Six, Call of Duty, a Battlefield squad, battle royale trios.
  • Substitutes wait their turn and move up by themselves, with a direct message, when someone drops out.
  • Each player's games: their roles, rank and in-game ID for every title they play.

Leagues

🏆 Run a league in your Discord

  • Teams with a name, tag, captain and players; teams can ask to enter with one button.
  • Fixtures made for you: every team plays every other, once or home and away, a week apart.
  • The table: points, wins, draws and losses, maps and form, with ties broken by head-to-head, then map and round difference.
  • A standings post that keeps itself up to date, with the week's matches.

Results

✅ Reported by one captain, confirmed by the other

  • The captain reports the maps or the series score, or that the other team didn't show.
  • The other team confirms or disputes it. With no dispute, it counts by itself after the waiting time you set.
  • Disputes go to your stewards with their own thread: set the result, keep it, or give a penalty, and both teams are told.

Free
