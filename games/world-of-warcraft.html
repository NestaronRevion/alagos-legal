Versions

Three versions, each its own game

A Classic character and a retail character are different characters with different classes and raids, so each version is switched on separately. A guild that plays two switches both on, and Alagos shows a game picker only then.

Sign-ups

⚔️ Press a role; Alagos picks the spec

The post keeps the buttons your guild knows: Tank, Healer and DPS, or Melee and Ranged too. A member presses the role they want, and Alagos signs them up with the spec of theirs that plays it.

  • The right spec, every time: their main spec first, then their off-spec, then another spec of their class, on their main character first and then their alts.
  • Everyone can see the comp: the post shows each name with its spec, like Nestaron · Holy Paladin.
  • A second choice: their off-spec's role, used if their role is full and that one has room.
  • A fair bench: a full role takes a bench in sign-up order, and people move up by themselves when someone drops out, with a DM to tell them.
  • Officers stay in control: move anyone between roles and the bench, and confirm who really came with attendance.

Characters

🧙 Members add their own characters

  • Main and alts, each with its realm, class, main spec and off-spec. The spec menus offer only that class's specs.
  • Item level, a Warcraft Logs link and a talent build (a talent string or a Wowhead or Raidbots link), for the officers to see.
  • Realms done right: two players named Arthas on two realms are two characters, shown as Arthas-Stormrage when your guild spans realms.
  • Your guild decides who adds characters: members freely (the default), members with an officer's approval, or officers only.

Raids

🐉 Every raid, picked by name

No template numbers to remember: press New event and pick Siege of Orgrimmar (25) or The Venomous Abyss (Mythic). The template fills in the type, the length and the role slots; you give the date and time.

  • Starting sizes you change for any night: 25-player 2 tanks, 6 healers, 17 damage; Mythic 2, 4, 14; flexible raids starting at 25 for any number you bring.
  • Role sets for each size, Melee and Ranged split if you like, plus dungeons, Mythic+, Delves, PvP and world bosses.
  • Your own templates for the nights you run every week.
  • The game's lists are data: a new raid or spec appears without waiting for an update, and your guild can add its own.

Free: the built-in templates and 3 of your ownPremium: unlimited templates, and hide the raids you don't run

Loot

🎁 Loot the way your guild runs it

  • Soft reserve, loot council and wishlists: reserves before the raid with the bot's rolls, council awards with a reason, and wishlists ranked by who has received least lately.
  • DKP, Zero-Sum DKP and EPGP: points earned from confirmed attendance, spent on open, sealed or fixed-price bids, with weekly decay where you want it. If attendance is corrected after the raid, the points follow.
  • Tier tokens know their classes: a token like Gauntlets of the Cursed Protector shows who can use it (Warrior, Hunter, Shaman, Monk), and the winner records the piece they took.
  • Never gold: Alagos does not run auctions for in-game currency.

Recruiting

📝 Applications to join

An Apply button on its own post or in your welcome flow. Applicants give their character, realm, class and specs, item level and logs, their raid experience and when they can raid, and your own questions.

  • Each application is posted in your officer channel with its own thread for discussion.
  • Accept, Accept with conditions, Bench or Deny. An approval gives your roles and puts them on the roster with their character. They're told by DM.
  • Your rules: how long before someone may apply again after a Deny (7 days by default), and whether a Deny shows your reason.

Free: one form, 3 of your own questions, the four outcomesPremium: 15 questions, several forms, officer voting, benched applicants found again for a role, export

Switching

📥 Bring your Raid-Helper history with you

Upload Raid-Helper's event export, or an event's JSON from its web view. Alagos brings each past raid in with every sign-up's answer, role, class and spec, matched to your members' characters, so your history is here on day one. You see a preview first; nothing is saved until you confirm, and importing a file twice adds nothing twice. It reads Raid-Helper's MoP, Retail and Forever templates. Free.
