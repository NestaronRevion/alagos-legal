Rotations

🚂 The Train: a fair Conductor and four carriages, every day

Alagos picks the day's Conductor and carriages from the players who have really been showing up, gives every eligible player a turn before anyone rides twice, and posts the result for officers to set in the game.

  • Who counts: active players, not on leave, with confirmed attendance at the events your league chooses, over the number of days your league chooses (default: Guardian, Krypton Rush, Gotham and Forge Breach over the last 7 days). Signing up doesn't count; only attendance an officer confirmed does.
  • The Conductor comes from your strongest players by power who have attended enough times.
    • You set how many attendances they need (default 2).
    • You set how many of your strongest players can be Conductor (top 15).
    • You set how many of those go first (the top 10).
  • Carriages need a minimum number of attendances (default 1). Seats are filled by preferred seat, then second choice, then no preference, then anyone eligible.
    • Members choose their seats in My profile. A member can do this once they have a character on the roster: either one they claimed after an officer added it, or one they added themselves where the league allows it. Officers can also set anyone's seats from the roster.
  • Your strongest players stay out of the carriages. The players in the Conductor group, and anyone who has already been Conductor this cycle, don't ride.
  • Ranks you leave out never ride (default: none).
  • Everyone gets a turn: nobody is picked twice until everyone eligible has had a go. The Conductor and the carriages each keep their own cycle.
  • Officers stay in control:
    • reroll any seat;
    • reset assigned players: pick anyone who can't take their seat, and Alagos finds replacements;
    • cancel a run, until the train departs 4 hours after it's set in the game.
  • Members are told. Once the train is marked set in the game, each rider can get a DM with their carriage.

🛡️ Guardian: every city's posts, filled fairly each week

Alagos fills each city's Guardian posts from the players who came, so the same handful of people aren't defending every week.

  • Who counts: two lists of event types, both chosen by your league.
    • Picked first: players who came to these (default: Guardian).
    • If needed: players who came to these (default: Gotham).
    • Also chosen by your league: how many days back to look (7), how many attendances someone needs (1), and which ranks are left out (default: Officer/R4; the Archon, R5, stays in).
  • Everyone gets a turn: nobody holds a second Guardian post until everyone eligible has held one.
  • City Guardians, one of two options:
    • Fixed: the same player is City Guardian every week.
    • Rotating: a group you name takes turns at all four of that city's posts. You can set a minimum rank or power for the City Guardian post. If nobody qualifies, last week's City Guardian keeps it.
  • Your cities, your way: add, rename and retire cities from Guild Settings. A retired city keeps its history.
  • Officers can reroll a post, reset the people already assigned, and tick each city off as it's set in the game.
  • Members are told. Once a city is marked set, each player in it can get a DM with their post.

⭐ Set every rule yourself

With Premium, the presets become a starting point. Open ✏️ Customise on either rotation and change any of these:

🚂 Train

  • How far back to look: 1 to 60 days. A short window rewards this week's regulars; a longer one is gentler on players who miss a week.
  • Which events count: any of the game's event types, your league's own types, or every event. For example, count only Gotham and Krypton Rush.
  • Attendances to be Conductor: 1 to 10.
  • The Conductor group: how many of your strongest players can be Conductor (up to 50), and how many of those are picked first.
  • Attendances for a carriage: 1 to 10.
  • Ranks left out of the train: any ranks you choose.

🛡️ Guardian

  • How far back to look: 1 to 60 days.
  • Who is picked first: any event types you choose, up to 25.
  • Who fills in if needed: a second list of event types, or none.
  • Attendances needed: 1 to 10.
  • Ranks left out: any ranks you choose, such as keeping officers in or leaving the Archon out.

⏰ Both rotations

  • They run by themselves. The train posts daily and the Guardian weekly, at the time you set. The fairness rules are the same as when an officer presses the button.
  • A day that has already been run by hand is skipped, so nobody gets two posts for one day.

Battle for Gotham

⚔️ City Hall and cannon teams, posted before every battle

Alagos posts your teams in the event's channel at the time you choose before a Gotham battle, pings the event's role, and keeps people in the same job week to week.

  • Who's on the post: everyone with confirmed attendance in the 30 days before the battle, plus anyone who signed up for it. Anyone who answered Won't Attend, is on leave or is inactive is left out.
  • City Hall and up to six cannon teams for an Ultimate Battle for Gotham, four for a Battle for Gotham, plus Reserves. The Ultimate and the Battle for Gotham each keep their own teams.
  • Copy for in-game mail: officers get each team as plain text, ready to paste for the members who aren't on Discord.
  • Rebuild the post when sign-ups change after it went out.
  • Set teams by hand: pick a job, then tick who holds it from name lists. Moving someone takes them off their old team.
  • Pin anyone who should never be moved.
  • An Emergency Response Team, picked from your City Hall and cannon players, for pulling a match back.

⭐ Automatic teams with Premium

  • One press builds every team from power and attendance over the last 90 days, most active placed first.
  • Newcomers are placed for you on each week's post, without moving anyone's usual job.
  • Your own City Hall rule: top power, a power range, or a spread across your roster, and how many players.
  • Your own cannon rule: evenly balanced, or tiered with Team A strongest; how many teams, and a size for each. Extras go to Reserves.
  • Which cannons each team covers, and a note for each.
  • Drift: players whose power has outgrown their team for a few weeks are flagged for a swap, which you approve.

Rewards

🏙️ Gotham and Krypton Rush seasons, with Red Box tracking

Who came, how often, and who has been given a Red Box, for every season. No more spreadsheets of who is owed one.

Gotham seasons

  • A season opens with its Battle for Gotham once its attendance is saved, and runs four weeks: the Battle for Gotham, then three Ultimates.
  • The report counts each separately: who came to the Battle for Gotham, and how many Ultimates each player attended, with a mark for who has already been given a box from each.
  • Award Red Boxes from the report. Alagos asks for your league's placement first and offers only what your league has left to give: 1 for the Battle for Gotham, and 2 for first place or 1 for second in the Ultimates. Only players who attended can be picked, one box each per round.

Absolute Krypton Rush seasons

  • Four-week seasons from the start date you set. Each week's Krypton Rush is typed Absolute or Greater automatically.
  • Boxes by result: enter your tier (Challenger, Elite or Legend) and placement, and Alagos works out how many your league can give, from 0 up to 5.
  • Anyone who attended at least once in the season can be given one.

Live in battle

⏱️ The battle tracker: City Hall time, live

One officer presses a button each time City Hall changes hands. Alagos keeps both sides' City Hall time, tells you whether you're ahead, and writes one-line call-outs short enough to relay in game chat.

  • Posts itself when staging opens and starts at the right moment, with no command to remember.
  • We took CH, They took CH, Missed, with Undo for a wrong press.
  • Correct lets you type the totals the game shows when you missed a few presses, and everything after follows them.
  • Each cycle's result: how long each side held it and the net, with each side's average and best hold.
  • A call-out on every capture, in its own channel, plus one when the other side can no longer win.
  • A forecast of where the match is heading at the current pace.
  • Stops at 40:00, when the game is decided.
  • The Nexus in a Battle for Gotham: press each side's Nexus speed as the game shows it, and City Hall time is counted at that speed.
  • A summary at the end, and practice runs that never count.
