Sign-ups

✨ Sign up by what you bring to the squad

  • Tank, Healer, Support and DPS buttons. In Guild Wars 2 a build fills any of them by its gear, so a member presses the role they're playing tonight and keeps their build: a Herald can be Support one night and Healer the next.
  • The post shows the squad: each name with their build.
  • Switch build from the answer only they see; their role stays.
  • A fair bench in sign-up order, moving up by itself, with a DM when someone gets in.

Characters

🧙 Every profession and elite specialization

  • All nine professions with their core build and four elite specializations each, Visions of Eternity's included: 45 builds.
  • Members add their own characters with their profession, build and a second build, their account name (Name.1234), a dps.report or Wingman link, and a build link from Snow Crows, Hardstuck or Metabattle.

Events

🐉 Raids, Fractals, World vs World and metas

  • Raid night and Guardian's Glade squads of 10: 1 tank, 2 healers, 2 support, 5 DPS, or a squad with no tank. The wings and the former Strike Missions are in the game's list.
  • Fractals of 5: 1 healer, 1 support, 3 DPS.
  • World vs World nights, meta trains and guild missions, with no fixed slots, or a 50-player World vs World squad.

Everything else

🎁 Loot, applications and your Raid-Helper history

  • Loot your way: soft reserve, loot council and wishlists free; DKP, Zero-Sum DKP and EPGP with Premium. Never gold: no auctions for in-game currency.
  • Applications to join: applicants give their profession, build, account name, raid and Fractals experience and when they can play. Accept, Accept with conditions, Bench or Deny, each with a thread for the officers.
  • Import from Raid-Helper, free: past raids and their sign-ups come with you, each with the role pressed and the build.
